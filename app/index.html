NusaStats is an independent statistics portal built for demonstration. Figures are published values from BPS (Badan Pusat Statistik) and the World Bank, latest complete year 2024 unless a year is stated, rounded for display.

Data 2019–2024 · No account, no tracking, no network requests — the whole site runs from its own files.
